# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while discussing evidence, accept
constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for users and the community.
Do not harass, threaten, insult, expose private information, or use sexualized
language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through a
GitHub security advisory. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
